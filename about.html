About Us

Who We Are

De-Bright Talented Kids School is a premier educational institution located along Sonitra Road, Amasaman, in the Greater Accra Region of Ghana. Since our founding, we have been dedicated to providing a holistic education that nurtures the intellectual, moral, and physical development of every child entrusted to our care.

From our Creche programme to Junior High School, we offer a seamless and stimulating educational journey that prepares students not just for national examinations, but for life itself. Our consistent 100% B.E.C.E pass rate is a testament to the hard work of our students and the dedication of our staff.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, God-fearing, and academically excellent environment where every child discovers their unique talents and develops the confidence to achieve their full potential.

Our Vision

To be the leading educational institution in Greater Accra, producing well-rounded, confident, and globally competitive graduates.

Our Values

Academic excellence, discipline, integrity, faith, and service to community form the bedrock of everything we do at De-Bright.

Our Community

We are more than a school — we are a family. Parents, teachers, and students work together to create a supportive learning community.

Our Programmes

Creche: For our youngest learners, we offer a warm, safe, and stimulating environment that encourages play-based learning, social development, and early literacy.

Primary School (1–6): Our primary curriculum follows the Ghana Education Service (GES) standards, supplemented with ICT classes, sports, cultural activities, and moral education.

Junior High School (JHS 1–3): Intensive academic preparation culminating in the Basic Education Certificate Examination (B.E.C.E). We maintain small class sizes to ensure individual attention.

Why De-Bright?

Choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you will ever make. At De-Bright, we offer: a dedicated and qualified teaching staff, modern ICT facilities, a school bus service covering major Accra routes, extra-curricular activities including sports, cadet corps, debate, and creative arts, and a proven track record of academic excellence.
